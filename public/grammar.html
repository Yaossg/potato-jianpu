Modern Jianpu Reloaded Grammar Reference

A concise reference for Modern Jianpu Reloaded source files, including headers, notes, durations, chords, ties, and tokenizer-level syntax.

Reference Details

Whitespace And Comments

Spaces and tabs may appear between tokens and are ignored. Line breaks separate header properties and score lines. Comments begin with ; and continue until the next line break. 

1 2 3 4 ; this comment is ignored
5 6 7 1'

Header Values

Header values are double-quoted strings. A backslash skips the following character while tokenizing, but the parser currently stores the raw inner text and does not unescape sequences. 

title: "Twinkle"
subtitle: "Traditional"
composer: "Traditional"
key: "C"
time: "4/4"
tempo: "120"
=
1 1 5 5 | 6 6 5 - |

Key, Time, And Tempo

Supported keys are C C# Db D D# Eb E F F# Gb G G# Ab A A# Bb B. One space inside the key value is ignored, and legacy accidental-first spellings such as #C and bB are accepted.

Time signatures use beatsPerMeasure/beatsPerWholeNote. Numerators are 2 3 4 6 9 12; denominators are 2 4 8 16. Tempo must be a positive number.

Notes, Accidentals, And Octaves

Pitch notes are 1 through 7. Rest is 0. Drum note is uppercase X. Accidentals before pitch notes are #, ##, b, bb, and %. Octave suffixes after pitch notes use ' and ,. 

#4 b7 %4 ##1 bb6
1' 2'' 6, 5,,

Durations

The default note duration is a quarter note. Dashes extend duration by quarter-note units. Slashes halve the duration. Dots add dotted rhythm. Dashes cannot be combined with slash/dot suffixes. 

1     = 1/4 whole note
1-    = 1/2 whole note
1--   = 3/4 whole note
1---  = 1 whole note
1/    = 1/8 whole note
1//   = 1/16 whole note
1.    = 3/8 whole note
1/.   = 3/16 whole note

Chords And Ties

Chords are enclosed in braces and contain pitch notes only. The duration suffix follows the closing brace and applies to the whole chord. Tie markers appear before the tied note. 

{1 3 5}
{#1 3 b5}/
1 ~1
3 - ~3/

Semantic Checks

  • Header key, time, and tempo values are normalized and validated after raw header parsing.
  • Measure durations are compared with the time signature after parsing and reported as warnings.
  • Ties are validated after parsing, using measure-local accidental persistence.
  • Accidentals persist within a measure for playback and tie validation, then reset at measure boundaries.

Railroad And EBNF

rule literal or token optional repeat

Parser Overview

The parser chooses header-document if any = token appears ahead in the file. Otherwise the same input is parsed as score-only notation. 

document        ::= NEWLINE* (header_document | score_document) EOF
header_document ::= header NEWLINE* score?
score_document  ::= score?

Header

header          ::= (NEWLINE | header_property)* EQUALS+ NEWLINE+
header_property ::= IDENTIFIER ":" STRING (NEWLINE | before EQUALS)

Semantic header fields are title, subtitle, composer, key, time, tempo, and hidden. Unknown fields are preserved in header.raw.

Score

score ::= (NEWLINE | line)*

Line

line  ::= label? BAR? measure (BAR measure)* BAR?
label ::= IDENTIFIER ":"

A single leading bar and a single trailing bar are accepted. Empty measures are still parse errors; for example, 1 2 || 3 4 creates an empty measure between the two bar tokens.

Measure

measure        ::= measure_octave? durable_note+
measure_octave ::= LOWER+ | HIGHER+

A measure octave prefix is consumed only at the start of the measure. It is one repeated token kind: ,+ or '+. Mixed prefixes are not one prefix.

Durable Note

durable_note ::= TIE? general_note duration?

Tie legality is validated after parsing. A leading ~ is syntactically accepted here, then rejected later if it is the first note, follows a rest, or does not match the previous sounding note.

General Note

general_note ::= pitch | REST | DRUM | chord

Pitch

pitch         ::= accidental? NOTE octave_suffix?
accidental    ::= SHARPSHARP | SHARP | FLATFLAT | FLAT | NATURAL
octave_suffix ::= LOWER+ | HIGHER+

Chord

chord ::= "{" pitch+ "}"

Chords contain pitch notes only. Rests, drum notes, duration suffixes before }, and empty chords are invalid.

Duration

duration ::= DASH+ | (HALF | DOT)+

Dash duration marks cannot be combined with slash or dot marks. The parser reports a specific duration error for forms such as 1-/ and 1/-.

Tokenizer

SKIPPED    ::= SPACE | TAB | COMMENT
IDENTIFIER ::= /[A-Za-z_]+/ except bb, b, X
STRING     ::= '"' (escaped-char | non-newline-non-quote)* '"'
NOTE       ::= "1" | "2" | "3" | "4" | "5" | "6" | "7"
REST       ::= "0"
DRUM       ::= "X"
BAR        ::= "|"
SHARPSHARP ::= "##"
SHARP      ::= "#"
FLATFLAT   ::= "bb"
FLAT       ::= "b"
NATURAL    ::= "%"
HALF       ::= "/"
DOT        ::= "."
DASH       ::= "-"
LOWER      ::= ","
HIGHER     ::= "'"
TIE        ::= "~"
EQUALS     ::= "="
NEWLINE    ::= "\n" | "\r\n" | "\r"
SPACE      ::= " "
TAB        ::= "\t"
COMMENT    ::= ";" characters-until-newline
