Modern Jianpu Markup Grammar Reference

A concise reference for Modern Jianpu Markup source files, including headers, notes, durations, chords, ties, and tokenizer-level syntax.

Reference

1. File Structure

A file has an optional header followed by score text.

title: "Twinkle"
key: "C"
time: "4/4"
tempo: "120"
=
1 1 5 5 | 6 6 5 - |

Score-only form is also valid:

1 2 3 4 | 5 6 7 1' |

If a file uses a header separator, all header fields must appear before it. Without a separator, the whole file is score text. Empty files are valid and use the default key, time, and tempo.

2. Whitespace And Comments

Spaces and tabs may appear between tokens and are ignored and optional when their absence does not cause ambiguity. For example, the following are all equivalent: 

1 2 3 4
5 6 7 1'
1234
5671'

Comments begin with ; and continue until the next line break. Comments are only for human readers and are ignored by the parser. For example, the following is valid and equivalent to the previous examples: 

1 2 3 4 ; this comment is ignored
5 6 7 1' ; this comment is also ignored

Line breaks that separate score lines and header properties are semantically-aware and not ignored, but consecutive line breaks are treated as a single one. 

1 2 3 4 5 6 7 1' ; this score is rendered in a single line 
1 2 3 4
         ; consecutive line breaks are treated as one
5 6 7 1' ; therefore, this score is rendered in two lines, not three

3. Header

Header fields use name: "value". The property name must contain ASCII letters or underscores. The value must be a double-quoted string on one line.

FieldDefaultMeaning
titleemptyRendered title
subtitleemptyRendered subtitle
composeremptyRendered composer
keyCKey used for playback and displayed metadata
time4/4Time signature
tempo120Beats per minute
hiddenemptyAny non-empty value hides rendered header metadata

Unknown header fields are allowed but have no built-in effect. Multiple = characters are accepted as the header separator. 

title: "Example"
===
1 2 3 4

4. Strings

Strings are delimited by ". A backslash skips the following character during tokenization, but the backslash is kept as written instead of being interpreted as an escape sequence. 

title: "A \"quoted\" title"

Unterminated strings are invalid:

title: "unterminated
=
1 2 3 4

5. Key

Supported keys:

C C# Db D D# Eb E F F# Gb G G# Ab A A# Bb B

One space inside the key value is ignored. Accidentals may also be written before the letter, as in #C and bB. Invalid keys are errors. 

key: "#C"
key: "bB"

6. Time

Time signatures use beatsPerMeasure/beatsPerWholeNote. Allowed numerators are 2 3 4 6 9 12. Allowed denominators are 2 4 8 16. Invalid time signatures produce an error. 

time: "4/4"
time: "6/8"
time: "2/16"

7. Tempo

Tempo must be a positive number. Invalid tempo values are errors.

tempo: "96"
tempo: "120"
tempo: "132.5"

8. Score Lines

A score line may optionally begin with a label. Labels use the same token form as header names: ASCII letters or underscores. The label is rendered at the left edge of that score line. 

verse: 1 2 3 4 | 5 6 7 1'

Measures are separated by |. A leading bar and a trailing bar are accepted. Each parsed measure must contain at least one note-like item. Empty measures are errors. 

| 1 2 3 4 | 5 6 7 1' |
1 2 || 3 4

Repeat barlines use |: to start and :| to end. They render as repeat barlines in SVG output. Playback, MIDI export, and audio export play the repeated section twice. 

|: 1 2 3 4 | 5 6 7 1' :|

9. Notes

Pitch notes are digits 1 through 7. Rest is 0. Drum note is uppercase X.

1 2 3 4 5 6 7
0
X

Lowercase x is an identifier token, not a drum note.

10. Accidentals

SyntaxMeaning
#sharp
##double sharp
bflat
bbdouble flat
%natural
 
#4 b7 %4 ##1 bb6

Accidentals apply only to pitch notes. Accidentals persist within a measure for playback and tie validation. They reset at each measure boundary.

11. Octaves

Octave suffixes appear after a pitch note. Each ' raises the note by one octave. Each , lowers the note by one octave. 

1' 2'' 6, 5,,

A measure may begin with a measure-level octave prefix. The prefix applies to all pitch notes in that measure. Per-note suffixes add to the measure prefix. The prefix must appear before any notes. Mixed prefix characters are not accepted as one prefix. 

'' 1 2 3 4
,, 5 6 7 1

12. Durations

The default note duration is a quarter note.

SyntaxDuration
11/4 whole note
1-1/2 whole note
1--3/4 whole note
1---1 whole note
1/1/8 whole note
1//1/16 whole note
1///1/32 whole note
1.3/8 whole note
1..7/16 whole note
1/.3/16 whole note

Dashes and slash/dot suffixes are mutually exclusive. Inputs such as 1-/ or 1/- are rejected with a specific duration diagnostic. Duration suffixes must follow a note, rest, drum note, or chord. Stray suffixes such as /1, .1, or -1 are rejected with a specific diagnostic. 

1 2/ 3// 4. 5/. 6- 7--

13. Chords

Chords are enclosed in braces. Only pitch notes are valid inside a chord. Rests and drum notes are not valid chord members. Empty chords are invalid. A closing brace without a matching { is invalid; for example, 1}2 reports the stray } as an unmatched chord end. 

{1 3 5}
{#1 3 b5}/
{1' 3' 5'}

The duration suffix belongs after the closing brace and applies to the whole chord.

{1 3 5}/
{1 3 5}-

14. Ties

A tie marker ~ appears before the tied note. The first note in a document cannot be tied.

1 ~1
3 - ~3/

A tied note must follow a sounding note. A tie after a rest is invalid. A tied pitch note must match the previous sounding pitch or chord after measure-local accidental persistence is applied. A tied drum note may tie to a previous drum note. The player merges tied notes into the previous melody event. 

Valid:
#4 ~4
{1 3 5} ~{1 3 5}
X ~X

Invalid:
1 ~2
#4 | ~%4
X ~1

15. Measure Duration Warnings

Each measure duration is compared with the active time signature:

expected = beatsPerMeasure / beatsPerWholeNote
  • 4/4 expects 1.
  • 3/4 expects 0.75.
  • 6/8 expects 0.75.

Mismatches produce warnings, not errors.

time: "4/4"
=
1 2

16. Error Summary

Errors are reported for:

  • unexpected characters
  • unterminated strings
  • malformed header fields
  • missing header separator when header mode is detected
  • invalid key, time, or tempo
  • empty measures
  • invalid note, chord, or tie syntax
  • mixed dash and slash/dot duration suffixes
  • stray duration suffixes
  • unmatched chord closing braces
  • first note tied
  • tied note after rest
  • tied note target mismatch

Warnings are currently used for measure duration mismatches.

Parser Overview

Leading newlines are ignored, then an optional header and optional score are parsed before end of file. 

file ::= NEWLINE* header? score? EOF

Header

header   ::= property* EQUALS+ NEWLINE+
property ::= label STRING NEWLINE+
label    ::= IDENTIFIER ":"

Semantic header fields are title, subtitle, composer, key, time, tempo, and hidden. Unknown fields are accepted but have no built-in effect.

Score

score ::= line (NEWLINE+ line)* NEWLINE*

Line

line              ::= label? start_barline? measure (measure_separator measure)* end_barline?
start_barline     ::= BAR | REPEAT_START
measure_separator ::= BAR | REPEAT_START
end_barline       ::= BAR | REPEAT_END
label ::= IDENTIFIER ":"

A single leading bar, repeat start, trailing bar, and repeat end are accepted. Empty measures are still parse errors; for example, 1 2 || 3 4 creates an empty measure between the two bar tokens.

Measure

measure ::= octave? durable_note+
octave  ::= LOWER+ | HIGHER+

A measure octave prefix is consumed only at the start of the measure. It is one repeated token kind: ,+ or '+. Mixed prefixes are not one prefix.

Durable Note

durable_note ::= tie? general_note length?
tie          ::= TIE

Tie legality is validated after parsing. A leading ~ is syntactically accepted here, then rejected later if it is the first note, follows a rest, or does not match the previous sounding note.

General Note

general_note ::= chord | rest | drum
rest         ::= REST
drum         ::= DRUM

Pitch

pitch_note ::= accidental? NOTE octave?
accidental ::= SHARPSHARP | SHARP | FLATFLAT | FLAT | NATURAL
octave     ::= LOWER+ | HIGHER+

Chord

chord ::= pitch_note | "{" pitch_note+ "}"

Chords contain pitch notes only. Rests, drum notes, duration suffixes before }, and empty chords are invalid.

Duration

length ::= DASH+ | (HALF | DOT)+

Dash duration marks cannot be combined with slash or dot marks. Forms such as 1-/ and 1/- are invalid.

Token Reference

Whitespace and comments are allowed between grammar tokens, so they are listed separately from tokens that become part of the written score. Reserved token spellings such as bb, b, and X are matched before identifiers. 

Ignored between tokens:
WS         ::= " " | "\t"
COMMENT    ::= ";" characters-until-newline

Structural tokens:
IDENTIFIER ::= /[A-Za-z_]+/ except bb, b, X
STRING     ::= '"' (escaped-char | non-newline-non-quote)* '"'
NOTE       ::= "1" | "2" | "3" | "4" | "5" | "6" | "7"
REST       ::= "0"
DRUM       ::= "X"
BAR        ::= "|"
REPEAT_START ::= "|:"
REPEAT_END   ::= ":|"
SHARPSHARP ::= "##"
SHARP      ::= "#"
FLATFLAT   ::= "bb"
FLAT       ::= "b"
NATURAL    ::= "%"
HALF       ::= "/"
DOT        ::= "."
DASH       ::= "-"
LOWER      ::= ","
HIGHER     ::= "'"
TIE        ::= "~"
EQUALS     ::= "="
NEWLINE    ::= "\n" | "\r\n" | "\r"
EOF        ::= end-of-file
