## Basic Notes

Numbered notes use `1` through `7`. Use `0` for a rest and `X` for a drum note.

<JianpuSource
  title="Basic note source"
  source={`1 2 3 4 | 5 6 7 0 | X X X X`}
/>

<ScorePreview title="Basic notes, rest, and drums" source={`1 2 3 4 | 5 6 7 0 | X X X X`} />

Lowercase `x` is not a drum note.

<JianpuExample
  title="Invalid drum spelling"
  status="invalid"
  description="Drum notes use uppercase X only."
  source={`1 2 x 4`}
/>

## Octaves and Accidentals

Octave suffixes come after a pitch note. A dot above or below the note changes the octave.

<JianpuSource title="Octave suffix source" source={`1' 2'' 3, 4,,`} />

<ScorePreview source={`1' 2'' 3, 4,,`} />

Each `'` raises the note by one octave; each `,` lowers it by one octave. Measure-level octave prefixes can also shift all pitch notes at the start of a measure. Per-note suffixes add to that measure prefix.

<JianpuSource
  title="Measure octave prefix source"
  source={`time: 3/4
===
' 1 2 3 | , 1 2 3`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  source={`time: 3/4
===
' 1 2 3 | , 1 2 3`}
/>

The prefix must appear before notes in the measure. Mixed prefix characters are not one prefix.

Accidentals come before a pitch note. `#` and `##` raise a pitch, `b` and `bb` lower it, and `%` cancels the active accidental for that note spelling.

<JianpuSource title="Accidental source" source={`#1 b2 %3 ##4 | bb5 - - -`} />

<ScorePreview source={`#1 b2 %3 ##4 | bb5 - - -`} />

Accidentals apply only to pitch notes. They persist within the current measure for playback, export, and tie validation, then reset at the next measure boundary.

Rests and drum notes cannot have octave marks or accidentals; adding those marks is invalid syntax.

<JianpuExample
  title="Invalid rest and drum modifiers"
  status="invalid"
  description="Accidentals and octave marks apply to pitch notes only, not rests or drums."
  source={`#0 X'`}
/>

## Chords

Chords are written with braces.

<JianpuSource
  title="Chord source"
  source={`{1 3 5} - {2 4 6} -`}
/>

<ScorePreview title="Rendered chords" source={`{1 3 5} - {2 4 6} -`} />

Chord members can use octave suffixes and accidentals. Rests and drum notes are not valid chord members. Put the duration suffix after the closing brace to apply it to the whole chord.

<JianpuSource title="Chord member source" source={`{#1 3 b5}/ {1' 3' 5'}- 0/ 0`} />

<ScorePreview source={`{#1 3 b5}/ {1' 3' 5'}- 0/ 0`} />

<JianpuExample
  title="Empty chord"
  status="warning"
  description="An empty chord is accepted, rendered as a rest, and reported as a warning."
  source={`1 {} 3 4`}
/>

<JianpuExample
  title="Invalid chord member"
  status="invalid"
  description="Chord members must be pitch notes, so rests and drums are rejected inside braces."
  source={`{1 0 X}`}
/>
