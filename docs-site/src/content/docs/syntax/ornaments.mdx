## Mordents

Modern Jianpu Markup currently supports upper and lower mordents. Write the ornament immediately after a single pitch note, before any duration suffix.

Use `(^)` for an upper mordent and `(v)` for a lower mordent.

<JianpuSource
  title="Mordent source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(^) 2(v) 3 - | 5(^)/ 5(v)/ 1' - 0`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Rendered mordents"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(^) 2(v) 3 - | 5(^)/ 5(v)/ 1' - 0`}
/>

The suffix comes before dashes, slashes, dots, or other duration marks.

<JianpuSource title="Mordents before duration suffixes" source={`1(^)- 1(v)/ 2(^).`} />

Mordents attach only to ordinary single pitch notes. They do not attach to rests, drum notes, chords, or tied notes in this version.

## Turns

Turns use the same post-note suffix position. Use `(z)` for a normal turn and `(s)` for an inverted turn.

<JianpuSource
  title="Turn source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(z) 2(s) 3 - | 5(z)/ 5(s)/ 1' - 0`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Rendered turns"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(z) 2(s) 3 - | 5(z)/ 5(s)/ 1' - 0`}
/>

Like mordents, turns attach only to ordinary single pitch notes and come before any duration suffix.

## Trills

Use `(tr)` for a trill. It follows the anchor pitch and comes before any duration suffix.

<JianpuSource
  title="Trill source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(tr) 2(tr) 3 - | 5(tr)/ 5(tr)/ 1' - 0`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Rendered trills"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(tr) 2(tr) 3 - | 5(tr)/ 5(tr)/ 1' - 0`}
/>

Trills attach only to ordinary single pitch notes. The upper auxiliary pitch follows the current key signature and any active accidental for that scale degree in the measure.

## Grace Notes

Grace notes are also written as post-note ornament suffixes. The suffix always attaches to the pitch note on its left. Use `(<...)` for grace notes rendered and played before the anchor note, and `(>...)` for grace notes rendered and played after it.

<JianpuSource
  title="Grace note source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(<2) 2(>3) 3(<45) 5(>6')`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Rendered grace notes"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
1(<2) 2(>3) 3(<45) 5(>6')`}
/>

Inside the grace-note suffix, write 1, 2, 4, or another power-of-two number of pitch notes using the normal accidental and octave syntax. A single grace note renders as an eighth note with one underline, two grace notes render as sixteenth notes with two underlines, four grace notes render with three underlines, and so on. Do not write rests, drum notes, chords, ties, dynamics, or duration marks inside the suffix.

Like mordents, grace notes attach only to ordinary single pitch notes in this version.

## Playback And Export

Playback, MIDI, WAV, and MP3 export expand each mordent into a short main-neighbor-main figure, followed by the remaining duration of the written note.

The upper mordent uses the next scale degree. The lower mordent uses the previous scale degree. The neighbor pitch follows the current key signature and any active accidental for that scale degree in the measure.

Playback, MIDI, WAV, and MP3 export expand turns into a short four-note figure, followed by the remaining duration of the written note. A normal turn plays upper-anchor-lower-anchor. An inverted turn plays lower-anchor-upper-anchor.

Playback, MIDI, WAV, and MP3 export expand a trill across the full written duration. It starts on the anchor, alternates with the upper auxiliary pitch at thirty-second-note density, and always keeps at least one complete anchor-upper pair.

Playback, MIDI, WAV, and MP3 export expand grace notes inside the written duration of the anchor note. Before-grace notes sound first, followed by the shortened anchor note. After-grace notes use the end of the anchor duration. Multiple grace notes split the grace duration evenly.

SVG, PNG, and PDF export render mordents, turns, and trills above the note using the bundled SMuFL ornament font. Grace notes render as small Jianpu figures beside the anchor note: before-grace notes on the left, after-grace notes on the right. Each grace figure has one or more short underlines whose lower edge connects to the anchor note with a quarter-circle arc. Downloaded SVG and PDF output embed the font needed for SMuFL ornament symbols.
