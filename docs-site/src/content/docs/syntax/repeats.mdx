## Repeat Barlines

Use `|:` to start a repeated section and `:|` to end it.

<JianpuSource
  title="Repeat barline source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | 5 6 5 - :|`}
/>

<ScorePreview source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | 5 6 5 - :|`} />

If a repeat end appears before any repeat start in the same line or track, playback treats the repeated section as starting at the beginning of that line or track.

## Adjacent Repeats

Use `:|:` when one barline ends the previous repeated section and starts the next one.

<JianpuSource
  title="Adjacent repeat source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 :|: 5 6 7 1' :|`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Adjacent repeat sections"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 :|: 5 6 7 1' :|`}
/>

## Repeated Endings

Repeated endings use bracket markers such as `[1]` and `[2]`. Non-final endings should end with `:|`; the final ending should be explicit and should not end with `:|`.

<JianpuSource
  title="Repeated ending source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | [1] 5 - 5 - :| [2] 1 - - -`}
/>

<ScorePreview source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | [1] 5 - 5 - :| [2] 1 - - -`} />

An ending can apply to multiple passes.

<JianpuSource
  title="Multi-pass ending source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | [1,2] 5 - 5 - :| [3] 1 - - -`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Multi-pass repeated ending"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | [1,2] 5 - 5 - :| [3] 1 - - -`}
/>

Ending pass lists can also be non-contiguous.

<JianpuSource
  title="Non-contiguous ending source"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | [1,3] 5 - 5 - :| [2] 3 - 3 - :| [4] 1 - - -`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Non-contiguous repeated endings"
  source={`time: 4/4
===
|: 1 2 3 4 | [1,3] 5 - 5 - :| [2] 3 - 3 - :| [4] 1 - - -`}
/>

Every repeated-ending group must include an explicit final ending. When more measures follow a final ending marker, only the first measure after that marker is treated as part of the ending.

## Diagnostics

<JianpuExample
  title="Missing final ending"
  status="invalid"
  description="A repeated-ending group cannot rely on unmarked following measures as the final pass."
  source={`|: 1 2 3 4 | [1] 5 - 5 - :|`}
/>

<JianpuExample
  title="Skipped ending number"
  status="invalid"
  description="Ending numbers must cover the pass order without skipping required earlier passes."
  source={`|: 1 2 3 4 | [1] 5 - 5 - :| [3] 1 - - -`}
/>

Repeated-ending validation is scoped to each repeat section and to each labeled track. Non-final endings return with `:|`; the explicit final ending closes the group without a repeat end.
