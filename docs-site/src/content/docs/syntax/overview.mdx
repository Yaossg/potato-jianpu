A document may have leading blank lines, an optional file header, optional score text, and then the end of file. A header is only recognized when the file uses a separator line containing exactly `===`. Header fields must appear before that separator; score, lyric, and track lines appear after it. Without a separator, the whole file is parsed as score text.

<JianpuSource
  title="Complete document source"
  source={`title: "Example"
composer: "Traditional"
time: 4/4
===
1 2 3 4 | 5 - 5 -`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Rendered overview example"
  source={`title: "Example"
composer: "Traditional"
time: 4/4
===
1 2 3 4 | 5 - 5 -`}
/>

The parser treats spaced and unspaced notation as the same grammar. Spaces and tabs may appear between score tokens. These two score bodies are equivalent:

<JianpuSource title="Compact score body" source={`1234|5-5-`} />

<JianpuSource title="Spaced score body" source={`1 2 3 4 | 5 - 5 -`} />

Comments begin with `;` and continue to the end of the line.

<JianpuSource
  title="Comment after a measure"
  source={`1 2 3 4 | ; a comment
5 - 5 -`}
/>

Blank lines and leading newlines are ignored. A document may contain only a score body, a header plus separator with no score body, or even be empty. Empty files are valid and use the default header values. Score-only documents do not need a separator.

<JianpuSource title="Score-only document" source={`1 2 3 4 | 5 - 5 -`} />

Measures are separated with <SyntaxToken token="|" label="barline" />. A line may begin with a barline or repeat start, and it may end with a barline or repeat end. Every measure must contain a note, chord, rest, or drum note. Consecutive bar tokens create an invalid empty measure; write an explicit `0` when the score needs silence.

<JianpuSource title="Invalid empty measure" status="invalid" source={`1 2 || 3 4`} />

Line breaks are meaningful because they separate header fields, score lines, and lyric lines. Consecutive blank lines are collapsed, so a blank line between two score lines does not create an extra rendered row.

<JianpuSource
  title="Blank line between score lines"
  source={`1 2 3 4

5 6 7 1'`}
/>

A line containing only `---` marks a manual page break. It is used by paginated SVG, PNG, and PDF export so the next score line starts on a new page; normal unpaginated preview and export ignore the marker. In labeled multi-voice scores, place `---` before the next `T1` line so the voice group stays together.

<JianpuSource
  title="Manual page break"
  source={`1 2 3 4
---
5 6 7 1'`}
/>
