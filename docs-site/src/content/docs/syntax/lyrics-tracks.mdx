## Lyric Lines

Lyric lines follow the score line they annotate. Use `L1:`-style labels when a score has multiple lyric lines.

## Track Labels

Track labels `T1:` through `T9:` and `T11:` through `T16:` write multi-track arrangements. `T10:` is reserved for MIDI drums and is not accepted as a score-line label.

<JianpuSource
  title="Two-track source"
  source={`time: 4/4
=
T1: 1 2 3 4 |
T2: 5 5 5 5 |
T1: 5 6 7 1' |
T2: 3 3 3 3`}
/>

<ScorePreview source={`time: 4/4
=
T1: 1 2 3 4 |
T2: 5 5 5 5 |
T1: 5 6 7 1' |
T2: 3 3 3 3`} />

When a score contains any labeled track, unlabeled score lines are treated as `T1`.

## Lyric Diagnostics

Lyrics are attached to the preceding score group. A lyric line before any score line is invalid. When a lyric line contains multiple whitespace-separated syllables and the main voice renders on one row, the renderer centers those syllables under sounding main-voice notes. Rests do not receive lyric positions. If the syllable count does not match the main voice note count, the lyric is rendered as a plain full line and reported as a warning.

<JianpuExample
  title="Mismatched lyric count"
  status="warning"
  description="The score still renders, but the lyric line has fewer syllables than notes."
  source={`1 2 3 4
L1: "one two"`}
/>

<JianpuExample
  title="Lyric before score"
  status="invalid"
  description="Lyrics must follow the score line they annotate."
  source={`L1: "too soon"
1 2 3 4`}
/>

## Track Grouping

Track lines are grouped in ascending label order and every labeled group starts at `T1`. A group may end early, so `T1 T2 T3 T1 T2` starts a second group after the first group finishes. `T1 T1 T2` is also valid because the second `T1` starts a new group. `T1 T2 T2`, `T1 T2 T2 T1`, or a group that starts at `T2` are invalid. Lines in the same group play together; the next group starts after the previous group ends.

<JianpuSource
  title="Grouped track source"
  source={`T1: 1 2 3 4
T2: 5 5 5 5
T3: 1 1 1 1
T1: 5 6 7 1'
T2: 3 3 3 3`}
/>

<ScorePreview
  title="Rendered grouped tracks"
  source={`T1: 1 2 3 4
T2: 5 5 5 5
T3: 1 1 1 1
T1: 5 6 7 1'
T2: 3 3 3 3`}
/>

## Header Labels
